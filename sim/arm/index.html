      Connect a real arm

      The sim becomes the arm's twin: it follows the real arm until you put it live.

      USB needs Chrome or Edge on a computer.

      From this page, ws://localhost works. A rosbridge on another computer needs wss://.

      Pose the real arm like the twin's home, then set home: base straight, upper arm leaning forward a little, forearm level, gripper pointing down and open.

      Joints that turn the wrong way:

      This arm is live. Take it off live before changing its hardware.

      Put the arm live?

      The real arm will move as its twin moves. Keep people and things out of its reach, and keep a hand near its power switch. The e-stop holds it where it is.
