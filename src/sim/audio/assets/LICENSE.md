Kenney's pack licence identifies Impact Sounds 1.0 (2019) as Creative Commons Zero. The Freesound item's licence is Creative Commons 0. Both were checked on 2026-09-28.

Edits: mono at 48 kHz; contacts high-passed at 65 Hz, trimmed to at most 450 ms with a 30 ms tail fade. Suction uses 5.00–11.25 seconds of the recording, high-passed at 110 Hz and low-passed at 5.5 kHz. The loader blends its last 250 ms across the start to make a six-second loop and normalises it. Opus uses 64 kbit/s for contacts and 80 kbit/s for suction; MP3 fallbacks use 96 and 128 kbit/s respectively. Procedural models in the parent directory are original project code.
