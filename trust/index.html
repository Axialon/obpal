02 · The connection

Check both screens show the same seal.

Especially when you type a code: compare all three symbols and their names on your phone and the screen you intend to control. If they differ, disconnect and pair again.

The seal is a short, 18-bit comparison aid for this connection. It is not proof that a site or build is legitimate. How it works.

04 · Copies and forks

A label has limits.

A fork or mirror can show an origin marker so you know you opened a different deployment. This helps honest forks explain themselves. A malicious copy can remove the marker. Check the domain and compare the connection seal yourself.
