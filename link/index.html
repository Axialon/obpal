Browser extension · Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Vivaldi, Arc

Your phone controls any website.

ob.Pal Link turns your phone into a gamepad for browser games, a 3D mouse for model viewers, or a keyboard for keyboard games, and with ob.Pal Desktop, for the programs on your PC you allow. Scan a QR code to pair; there's no app to install.

Download ob.Pal Link How to install

Version 1.2.0 · free, MIT licensed · Chrome Web Store listing on its way

Controller

A standard gamepad for any game that uses the Gamepad API, cloud gaming included. Rumble reaches your phone.

3D

Drag to rotate, two fingers to pan, pinch to zoom, on any 3D viewer in the page.

Keys

Sticks become WASD and the arrow keys, buttons become Space, Enter and the rest, and the right stick moves the mouse.

PC

Keyboard and mouse for the desktop programs you allow, one by one, through ob.Pal Desktop on Windows. Ctrl+Alt+Backspace stops everything.

1

Unzip

Unzip obpal-link.zip into a folder you'll keep.

2

Load it

Open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and pick that folder.

3

Pair

On the page you want to control, click the ob.Pal Link icon. Scan the code with your phone, then turn on This tab.

PC control on Windows

Download ob.Pal Desktop, unzip it into a folder you'll keep and double-click install.cmd. In the extension, pick PC and click Control the whole PC: your phone becomes this computer's mouse and keyboard. Tap to click, hold to right-click, two fingers to scroll. Or allow one program at a time. Ctrl+Alt+Backspace stops everything at once.

Updating? Switch the extension away from PC, then unzip the new version over the old folder.

Works without internet

A phone you've paired once connects straight over your Wi-Fi when the internet is down: the popup shows a direct code, and the controller opens from your phone's cache.

Private by design

No accounts and no analytics. Input goes from your phone to your computer over an encrypted connection, and the extension only acts in the tab you switch on, or the programs you allow. Privacy policy
