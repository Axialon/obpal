ob.Pal

Open-source phone remote

Your phone is the remote for anything 3D.

Scan a QR code and your phone steers what's on screen: turn it to rotate objects, point to aim, swipe to orbit. There's no app to install on either device.

Open the viewer on this screen How it works
1

Open the viewer

On your computer, TV browser or headset, open this site's viewer. A QR code appears.

2

Scan with your phone

Use the phone's normal camera. The remote opens in your browser. No app or account needed.

3

Hold and turn

Hold the big button and turn your phone to rotate the model. Swipe, pinch and twist on the trackpad.
